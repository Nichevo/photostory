    3. Check the photos

    Click a photo below a trip to add it, or click a chosen photo to remove it. Green tags mark photos where both of you were recognised. To make a special moment stand out, such as getting engaged or dinner with family, type it under that photo as a highlight. Highlights get a coloured frame and label in the PDF, and are listed at the top of the trip page and on the cover.
