    3. Check the photos

    Cover page: photo and map

    The cover shows one photo of you both, above a map of all your trips. Click a photo to use it on the cover.

    Click a photo below a trip to add it, or click a chosen photo to remove it. Green tags mark photos where both of you were recognised. Give a photo a caption, and click Make highlight for special moments such as getting engaged or dinner with family. Highlights get a coloured frame and label in the PDF, and are listed at the top of the trip page and on the cover.

    Help me recognise you

    I wasn't sure about these faces. Telling me who they are makes the photo choices better, and I'll remember it for next time. If it's someone important to your story, like family, choose Someone else important and give their name: photos of you both with them will be preferred.
